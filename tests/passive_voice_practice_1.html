Present & Past Simple Passive

4 упражнения · 10 заданий в каждом · проверка ответов и объяснения к каждому пункту.

Present Simple Passive am / is / are + V3 (Past Participle)
English is spoken all over the world.
These books are written by famous authors.
Past Simple Passive was / were + V3 (Past Participle)
The house was built in 1990.
The windows were cleaned yesterday.
