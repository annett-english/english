Exercise 2 — True or False

Click True or False for each statement.

1. Queen Victoria was the first monarch to live in Buckingham Palace.

Answer: True

2. Buckingham Palace has more rooms than Windsor Castle.

Answer: False — Windsor Castle has even more rooms.

3. There are around 350 clocks in the palace.

Answer: True

4. If you see the Royal Standard flag, it means the king is not home.

Answer: False — the Royal Standard means the king IS home.

5. Queen Elizabeth II liked to spend weekends at Windsor Castle.

Answer: True

6. During Elizabeth's reign, you could book tickets to see the state rooms in December.

Answer: False — it was in August and September.
