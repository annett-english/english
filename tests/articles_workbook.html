a / an

  • A1 ед.ч. исчисл. сущ. — неопределённое: a house
  • A2 a/an + прил. + сущ. ед.ч.: a nice house
  • A3 первое упоминание в истории
  • A4 what a/an + сущ.!: what a day!
  • A5 once a week / twice a month
  • A6 a/an + профессия: I am a student
  • A7 устойчивые: a bit, a lot (of), a few, a little, a couple of, a number of, a kind/type of, have a look/rest, take a break, have a good time, in a hurry

the

  • T1 второе упоминание
  • T2 что-то конкретное: open the door
  • T3 уникальное: the sun, the moon, the Internet
  • T4 места: go to the shop
  • T5 превосходная степень: the biggest
  • T6 устойчивые: the same, the next/previous, the other, the only, at the moment, in the end, by the way, on the other hand, in the morning/afternoon/evening, at the beginning/end

нулевой артикль

  • Z1 обобщение: Women talk more than men
  • Z2 home/work/school/university/church + at/to/from
  • Z3 дни недели и месяцы
  • Z4 next/last/this + day/week/year
  • Z5 устойчивые: go to bed, be in bed, by bus/car/train/plane, on foot, at night, all day/night, face to face, have breakfast/lunch/dinner
