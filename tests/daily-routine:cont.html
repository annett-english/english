Present Continuous — то, что происходит сейчас:
＋ I am / he is / we are + V-ing — She is brushing her teeth.
－ am / is / are + not + V-ing — I am not checking my phone.
？ Am / Is / Are + … + V-ing? — Are they having breakfast?
Орфография: get → getting, have → having, wake → waking, tidy → tidying.
Привычки и расписание — Present Simple: I usually get up at seven.
