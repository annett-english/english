📘 Правило

Making suggestions

Мы используем Why not или Why don't you + инфинитив без to, чтобы сделать предложение:

  1. Why not try connecting with people in other industries?
  2. Why don't you give more information about your latest project?

Мы используем What about и How about + форма -ing:

  1. What about changing your profile picture?
  2. How about giving more details about the website you designed?

Giving advice

Мы используем should, shouldn't, ought to и could + инфинитив, чтобы дать совет:

  1. You should give detailed information about your experience.
  2. You shouldn't use words like 'passionate' on your profile.
  3. You could include information about your interest in hiking.
  4. You ought to think outside the box.
