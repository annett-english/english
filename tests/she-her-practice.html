✍️ She / Her / His / Our — A1

Напоминание

She / He / They / We — кто делает. После них идёт глагол: She likes pizza. He is Tom.

Her / His / Their / Our — чей. После них идёт существительное: her car, his name, our house.

КтоЧейПример
ImyI like my car.
hehisHe is Tom. His car is red.
sheherShe is Sarah. Her name is Sarah.
weourWe like our house.
theytheirThey like their dog.

✗ Her likes pizza. → ✓ She likes pizza.   ✗ She name is Kate. → ✓ Her name is Kate.
