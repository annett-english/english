Напоминание

Comparative + than

old → older, big → bigger, easy → easier, modern → more moderngood → better · bad → worse · far → further / farther

(not) as … as · the same as

She’s not as tall as me.Her dress is the same as mine.После than / as: me, him, her или I am, he is. She’s taller than I.

less … than = менее … чем

This car is less expensive than that one.He drives less carefully than his father.

Superlative

the tallest · the most interesting · the best · the worst · the furthestAdverbs: the fastest · the most carefully · the bestthe least + adj / adv = наименее

Likely vs probably

be likely to + verb: It’s likely to rain.probably + verb: I’ll probably stay at home.probably won’t + verb
