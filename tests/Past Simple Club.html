Правило на одной странице

was / were

I / he / she / it → was
you / we / they → were

  • She was tired after work.
  • They were at a cozy café yesterday.
  • She wasn't at home. / They weren't busy.
  • Was she at the gym? Were they at the café?

did + глагол (V1)

Отрицание и вопрос: did + начальная форма.

  • She cleaned her room yesterday.
  • She didn't study. (не studied!)
  • Did she clean her room?

Неправильные глаголы

В утверждении — 2-я форма, после did — снова V1.

  • wake → woke · buy → bought
  • drink → drank · wear → wore
  • go → went · eat → ate
