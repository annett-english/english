⏱️ How long does it take?

Говорим о том, сколько времени занимает дорога или поездка.
take + person + time + to get (to/from)
1. It takes me 20 minutes.
2. It takes him an hour.
3. How long does it take?
4. How long did it take?
Present Simple: takes
Past Simple: took
Future Simple: will take
