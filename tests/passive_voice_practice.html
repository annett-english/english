Формулы (шпаргалка)

ВремяPassive formПример
Present Simpleam/is/are + V3The book is read by many students.
Present Continuousam/is/are + being + V3The book is being read by Sarah now.
Past Simplewas/were + V3The house was built in 1990.
Past Continuouswas/were + being + V3The film was being watched at 8 p.m.
Present Perfecthave/has + been + V3The homework has been finished.
Past Perfecthad + been + V3The letter had been sent before he arrived.
Future Simple (will)will + be + V3The email will be sent tomorrow.
Be going toam/is/are + going to + be + V3The cake is going to be baked tomorrow.
Modal verbsmodal + be + V3The documents must be signed today.
