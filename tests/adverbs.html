Adjective → Adverb
+ -lyslow → slowly
y → -ilyhealthy → healthily
-le → -lypossible → possibly
irregulargood → well; fast, hard
very / quite / really / incredibly

Усиливают прилагательное или наречие и стоят перед ним.

The exams are quite difficult. · She drives incredibly fast.
