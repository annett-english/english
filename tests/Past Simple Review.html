Повторение: три формы Past Simple

Утверждение (+)

I / he / she / it → was
you / we / they → were

  • She was tired after work.
  • She cleaned her room. (V + ed)
  • She woke up early. (2-я форма)

Отрицание (−)

wasn't / weren't
didn't + V1

  • They weren't busy.
  • She didn't cook dinner.
  • She didn't wear a dress.

Вопрос (?)

Was / Were + подлежащее
Did + подлежащее + V1

  • Was she at the gym?
  • Were they at the café?
  • Did she buy fresh flowers?
